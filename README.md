# AdoptiPet

🐾 Introducing AdoptiPet — my university project, built to give more animals a second chance.

As part of my university project, I've been designing and building AdoptiPet, a mobile app that connects shelters, adopters, and donors in one place — prototyped entirely in FlutterFlow.

What it does: 🐶 Browse adoptable animals with search & filters 📍 Discover shelters near you, with full details and contact info ❤️ Follow up on your adopted companions and track their progress over time 💰 Support shelters directly through a built-in donation system with funding progress bars 🛒 A product marketplace for pet food, supplies, and essentials ✍️ Simple tools for shelters to add, edit, and manage animal listings

The goal is simple: make adoption easier to discover, easier to support, and easier to follow through on — for both shelters and the people who want to help.

Still a work in progress, but I'm really proud of how it's shaping up as a project. Grateful for the chance to combine no-code development with a cause I care about. More updates soon 👇
